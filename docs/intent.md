# Intent

## Problem
Factories run in three shifts. Each shift records production, downtime, quality and maintenance in
Excel, PDFs, paper or chat messages. The next shift has to read all of it to learn what happened.
Details get lost, abnormal numbers go unnoticed, pending maintenance is forgotten, and managers have
no consolidated view.

## Goal
Give the incoming shift one trustworthy page: what happened, what is abnormal, and what to do first,
with every statement traceable to the original record.

## Who it is for
- Incoming shift supervisor (primary): needs a prioritised checklist in under a minute.
- Plant manager: wants trends and repeated failures across shifts.
- Maintenance and QA leads: want only the items that concern them.

## Principles
1. **Numbers are never generated by an LLM.** Totals, achievements and anomalies are computed by code.
2. **Show your evidence.** Each flag and answer cites the numbers and the source file/row.
3. **Humans decide safety.** ShiftMind lists what to verify; it never says a machine is safe to run.
4. **Degrade gracefully.** Everything works with no API key; AI only improves wording.
5. **Reject, don't guess.** Unreadable rows are reported to the user rather than silently fixed.

## Out of scope for v0.1
Authentication, multi-plant tenancy, OCR for scanned pages, live PLC/SCADA integration, push alerts.
